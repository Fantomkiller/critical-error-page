## Prompt grafiki hero

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide website hero background for a World of Warcraft guild, inspired by The War Within's Dornogal and Earthen city architecture. Cinematic panoramic view of a monumental dwarven stone city inside a vast mountain cavern, strong stacked geometric buildings, great bronze-framed stone archways, distant forge windows with warm amber lights, subtle teal mineral reflections and immense pillars disappearing into soft atmospheric haze. Rich painterly fantasy game concept art, dignified and welcoming, muted slate blue and basalt gray with antique gold and warm amber accents. Gentle broad lighting, soft detail, no harsh sharpened edges, no red sky, no lava, no exaggerated glow. Composition: wide landscape 16:9, architectural focal point and bright city vista on right two-thirds; left third is darker calm atmospheric stone and shadow with low detail for large text overlay. Preserve dimensional visual depth. No text, no logo, no UI, no foreground characters, no watermark. Finished elegant website background.
