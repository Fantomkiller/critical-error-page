## horizontal-emblem

Plik: `horizontal-emblem-v1.png`

Prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Create a richly designed original World of Warcraft raiding guild logo with exact text "CRITICAL ERROR". Transparent background. IMPORTANT composition: a WIDE LOW HORIZONTAL EMBLEM, approximately 3.5:1 width to height, like an ornate fantasy nameplate or forged guild banner. The words are on ONE line and structurally integrated into the emblem, NOT plain typography with a tiny icon. Decorative pointed metal frame embraces the lettering above, below and on both sides. A restrained small broken raid warning rune and a cracked blade or fracture through the central frame suggest a critical raid mistake. Readable bold custom fantasy lettering, ivory silver metal with dark iron depth, crimson inner accents, tasteful warm highlights. Embellished and crafted, strong Warcraft raid identity, balanced ornamentation, polished dimensional illustration with crisp edges. Typography remains the main focus and occupies most of the emblem. No tall shield, no giant symbol above text, no dwarven stones or Dornogal motif, no skull mascot, no scenery, no mockup, no slogan, no extra words. Wide landscape canvas with generous transparent margins around the complete horizontal silhouette.
